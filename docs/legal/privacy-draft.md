This policy explains what AgentoolRank ("we"), operated by TENSO LLC (Colorado, USA), collects and why. You can browse AgentoolRank without an account; there is no sign-in.

## What we collect

**Anonymous visit statistics.** We run our own simple visit counter instead of a third-party analytics service. When you load a page or click certain links, your browser sends us the event name, the page path, the site that referred you, any `ref` or `utm_*` tag in the link you arrived from, and your country as reported by our hosting provider. Events are linked by a random ID kept in your browser tab's session storage (not a cookie), which is discarded when you close the tab. They contain no name, email address or IP address, and we do not share them. Known bots are not recorded. We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

**Weekly digest.** If you subscribe, we store your email address and send you the weekly digest. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link; unsubscribing stops the emails.

**Tool submissions.** If you submit a tool (through the website, our API or our MCP server), we store what you enter: your name, your email address, the tool's website, tagline and GitHub URL, the listing option you choose, and where you came from (referrer or `ref` tag). We use your email only to tell you about your submission's review and status. A private status link lets you check your submission.

**Payments.** Paid listing options are one-time payments processed by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and never reach our servers. We store the option you bought, the amount, the Stripe checkout session ID and the referral source, so we can apply your listing and reconcile payments.

**AI search.** If you use the AI search box, your question is sent to a large-language-model provider (through OpenRouter) to generate the answer. Your past conversations are stored only in your own browser (local storage); we do not store them on our servers.

**Messages to tool maintainers.** For tools already listed, we may send one email to a contact address the maintainer publishes on their own website or repository, to point them to their tool's page. We do not send follow-ups, and if you reply "no" we will not email you again.

**API and MCP requests.** Requests to our public API and MCP server are processed to return data; we do not require or store an account.

## Service providers

Our hosting provider (Vercel) processes technical request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the service from abuse. Our database provider (Turso) stores the data described above. Emails are delivered through our email provider (Brevo), which receives the recipient's address and the message. Payments are handled by Stripe. AI search questions are processed by the model provider described above.

## Deleting your data

Email hello@agentoolrank.com from the address concerned and ask us to delete it. We do it within 30 days and confirm by email. Stripe keeps payment records as required by law.
